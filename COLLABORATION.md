# Invitation to research collaborators

**Originating concept and initial proposal: Tonino Schäfer, independent proposer.**

I am seeking an experimental optics or quantum-interference research group interested in assessing and, if justified, performing a controlled version of my proposed **complete co-rotating double-slit experiment**.

The difference from a rotating slit plate is that **laser, slits, detection screen and camera are mounted together and moved as a single apparatus**. The pilot question is whether any fringe-visibility change remains once imaging, vibration, bending, airflow and expected inertial effects are quantified and ruled out as explanations.

The motivation is my conjecture that particle-associated physical wakes or traces might contribute to interference. This is **a speculative hypothesis, not an established discovery**. Even a puzzling optical result would not by itself demonstrate the conjecture or wavefunction collapse. Null results are welcome.

## Contributions sought

- Review the optics, feasibility and closest prior literature.
- Specify an adequate optical model, controls and rig design.
- Provide expertise or laboratory facilities for a safe precision experiment.
- Preregister and analyze the data with independent replication if warranted.
- Consider a separately designed single-photon or matter-wave test where scientifically useful.

## Recognition and participation

I would like to be appropriately credited as the proposer of the concept and to participate in designing and interpreting any resulting experiment. I welcome substantial contributions from other researchers; future scientific authorship and contributor roles should fairly reflect each person's actual work and the relevant journal standards.
